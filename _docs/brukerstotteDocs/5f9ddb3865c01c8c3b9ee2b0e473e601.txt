Help and guides for Common ICT solutions
On the website for help and guides for Common ICT solutions you will find information about ID-porten, the common contact register, digital mailbox, eSignering and eInnsyn.

ID-porten help desk provides guidance regarding Common ICT solutions and help to log in through ID-porten.

The Norwegian Digitalisation Agency is responsible for ID-porten.
