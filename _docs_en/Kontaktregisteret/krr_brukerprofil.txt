Introduction
Companies using the contact register can forward a logged in user to
In order for the citizen to keep their contact information in KRR updated, they will have to confirm the information by logging through the ID port every 90th day.
It is also possible to log directly to your contact information service.
How:
Ask Digdir to enter your integration.  This happens p.t. Manually, but will hopefully soon become self -catering.
API access
Hopefully, it will soon be possible for selected companies to change the mobile/email directly into the contact register, via API-Kall.
Note that such solutions must clearly inform to the citizen that:
* The changes occur in the national contact register and not only apply to the individual agency
* Change of mobile number can affect SMS-based login with MinID
