Authentication methods in the machine gate
Companies can authenticate in the machine gate with a business certificate or asymmetrical keys, which are their business digital ID.
What is the purpose of a business certificate/keys?
Virility certificate/keys are used to sign the request against the machinery. The machine gate then checks the validity of the certificate/key. This is described in more detail on JWT Grant since.
Lifetime and validity for business certificate/keys?
A key has a maximum of one year of life from the time it is posted. Business certificate has three years of life.
If a business certificate is used for authentication to our services, it must be a valid Norwegian X.509 certificate.
Can foreign companies order a business certificate?
One must be registered in the Norwegian Unit Register to order a business certificate.
Who can issue business certificates?
As of today, BuyPass and Commfides are approved by Nkom for unsteading business certificates.
We refer to Norway's trust list for updated information on who can issue business certificates.
Link to issuers:
Buypass: https://www.buypass.no/produkter/skervikkabernssertifier-esegl

How is asymmetrical keys created?
There are different software for creating asymmetrical keys. This will not be discussed in the Directorate of Digitization's technical documentation.
Do you use the same certificate for testing as in production?
No, in the test environments you have to have a test business certificate. This is also ordered from Buypass and Commfides.
Buypass: https://www.buypass.no/produkter/skervikkabernssertifier-esegl
Commfides: https://www.commfides.com/bestilling-vests certificateseid20/
Can you reuse a business certificate/keys on several clients in the machine gate?
Yes, you can use the same certificate/key on several clients in the machine gate.
Does the machine gate support the new SEID 2.0 certificates?
Yes, the machine gate supports SEID 2.0 certificates from both Buypass and Commfides.
Management of certificate/keys
We strongly advise against sharing client registrations (with secrets/keys) across multiple installations. For suppliers and companies with many integrations, also recommend using asymmetrical keys instead of business certificates. For safety reasons, suppliers should not manage customers' business certificate/keys.
