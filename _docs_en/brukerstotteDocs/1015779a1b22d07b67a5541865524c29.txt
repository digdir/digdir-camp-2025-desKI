Why do I have to choose between two mailboxes?

The public has entered into an agreement with two suppliers of digital mailbox, digipost and e-box. The mailboxes are equal and both meet strict safety requirements. You can choose which digital mailbox you want. You can have both e-box and digipost, but you can only get mail from the public to one of them. You can choose this from your user profile on norge.no.
