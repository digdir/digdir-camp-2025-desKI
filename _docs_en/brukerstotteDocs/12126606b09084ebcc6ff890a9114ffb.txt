How do I get a mailbox from Digipost?

To select digital mailbox from Digipost:

Go to the website www.norge.no
Click "Select Digital Mailbox"
Click "Go to Digipost"
To create a digital mailbox from Digipost, you must identify yourself electronically with BankID or Buypass ID.
To read mail from the public, you must use the ID port

If you have any questions, contact digipost: kundeservice@digipost.no or tel. (+47) 22 03 00 00

Email

brukerstotte@digdir.no

NB! Do not send sensitive information about yourself and your electronic ID by email.
