"An error has occurred"
The error message "An error has occurred" may come from using a favorite link or bookmark to go straight to the website.
Often you need to delete cookies in your browser. You can find guidance on how to do it here.
It may be due to an error in the link you use.
You can get the error if you use the navigation buttons (back and forth) in the browser during login.
The error message will also occur if you have been automatically chopped due to inactivity. You must then restart the login.
