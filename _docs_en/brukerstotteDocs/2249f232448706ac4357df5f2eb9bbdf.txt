Reserve you for communication online

The reservation will apply to:

decision
advance notice under section 16 of the Public Administration Act
messages that are crucial to your legal position or for the processing of a case 
messages like that for other reasons are very important that you get

When reserving you, letters containing important information will just be sent to you on paper. For example, it may be the tax settlement, a letter on the allocation of kindergarten space, building permit or decision on benefits from NAV. If you have reserved you, it applies to the entire public sector, both the state and the municipality.

Due to mailing, you will receive the letters later than if the letter is sent digitally.

If you reserve against digital communication, you will still be able to log into public services on the Internet.
