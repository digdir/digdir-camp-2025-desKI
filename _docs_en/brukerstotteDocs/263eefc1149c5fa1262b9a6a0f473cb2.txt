What can I not reserve?

State and municipality can send you important notice and messages even if you have reserved digital communication. You will receive such notice on the mobile number or email address you have entered into the contact and reservation register.  For example, there may be reminders of agreements or notice of water closure.

Email

brukerstotte@digdir.no

NB! Do not send sensitive information about yourself and your electronic ID by email.
