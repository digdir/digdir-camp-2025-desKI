A joint contact and reservation register
When you log in to a public service on the Internet, you are asked to enter your mobile number and email address. The mobile number and e-mail address you enter yourself will be part of a joint contact and reservation register for the state and municipality.

This information uses the state and municipalities to notify you of decisions and other important letters sent to you digitally. You will also be able to receive service messages and information by SMS or e-mail. For example, there may be reminders of agreements or notice of water closure.
