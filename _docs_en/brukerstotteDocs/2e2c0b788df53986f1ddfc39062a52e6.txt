Recovery of contact information

Norwegian Digitalisation Agency may remove your contact information on suspicion of identity theft or abuse.

If your contact information (phone number, email address and digital mailbox) has been deleted in the contact and reservation register, you must log in with an electronic ID to re-register contact information.

If you have an asset agreement with BankID, Buypass or Commfides, you can re -register contact information by logging in to norge.no

If you want to use MinID, please contact the ID port support (+47) 800 30 300.
