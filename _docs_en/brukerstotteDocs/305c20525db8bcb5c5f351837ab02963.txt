How do I log into Digipost to read public mail?

To read mail from the public, you must choose to log in with the ID port.

How to log in:

Go to www.digipost.no and click "Log In"
Click the red field "Log in via the ID port"
Choose one of the login methods
Email

brukerstotte@digdir.no

NB! Do not send sensitive information about yourself and your electronic ID by email.
