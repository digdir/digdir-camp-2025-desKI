Different security levels

The digital services have different levels of security. It is the business that owns the service that determines what security level their service should have - significant level of security (level 3) or the highest level of security (level 4). This is determined based on the consequences of unfortunate events for the user and the business.

Significant security level
MinID provides login to services with a significant level of security (level 3). It has a two-factor security solution. MinID consists of birth number and personal password, as well as disposable codes by SMS or Push notification from the MinID app.

Highest security level 
BankID, Buypass and Commfides deliver electronic ID at the highest security level (level 4). This is a two-factor solution, where one must also meet personally to legitimize yourself. This is to prevent the safety element from falling into the wrong hands. 

With the highest level of security, you get access to health information. If you are logged in with MinID, but want to log in to a service that requires a higher level of security, you must log in again with BankID, Buypass or Commfides.
