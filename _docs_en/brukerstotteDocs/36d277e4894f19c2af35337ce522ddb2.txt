Information about others

If you have received information about other than yourself after logging in to a public service with the ID port, send an email to brukerstotte@digdir.no

It is also important that you do not spread this information further. You also have a duty of confidentiality about who you were logged in as.

Even if you have inadvertently access the information and cannot be blamed for it, it can be punishable to share all or part of this information further. If you have downloaded such information, you must delete it immediately. Any prints must be shredded. 

Email

brukerstotte@digdir.no

NB! Do not send sensitive information about yourself and your electronic ID by email.
