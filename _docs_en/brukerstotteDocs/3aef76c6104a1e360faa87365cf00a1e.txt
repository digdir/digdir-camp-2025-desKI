"Your MinID user has a failure condition that makes you can't log in"

"Your mini-user has an error condition that allows you to not log in. Contact User Support for more information."

Reasons for you to get this error message:

Your MinID user has not been in use for more than 2 years, and has thus been closed
You even asked for your MinID user to be closed
You've got a new identification number and try to log in with your old number (you need to order a new mini -Active letter to your new birth number)
Your contact information in the contact and reservation register has been removed. You can read more about it here.
