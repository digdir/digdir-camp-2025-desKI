Do you not receive one-time code by SMS or email

If you do not receive codes by SMS or e-mail, you must contact the ID port support. We must then reset your user and send you a new minide activation letter so that you can register a new user. You can do this by calling the ID port support at (+47) 800 30 300.

By reset:

Do you receive activation letters within 10 working days (inward in Norway)
The letter of activation is sent to the address you are registered with in the National Register. If you have both a postal address and a residential address, it will be sent to the postal address.
