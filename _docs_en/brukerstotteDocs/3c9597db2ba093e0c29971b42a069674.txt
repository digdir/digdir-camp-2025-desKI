How do I get a mailbox from e-box?

To select digital mailbox from e-box: 

Go to the website www.norge.no
Click "Select Digital Mailbox"
Click "Go to E-Box"
To create a digital mailbox from e-box, you must identify yourself electronically with BankID
To read mail from the public, you must use the ID port

If you have any questions, contact e-box on a contact form or call tel. (+47) 22 89 85 04

Email

brukerstotte@digdir.no

NB! Do not send sensitive information about yourself and your electronic ID by email.
