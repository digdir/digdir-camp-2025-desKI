How do I know I have received mail digitally from the public?

The public will notify you at the email or mobile number you have registered in the contact and reservation register.  

You can check your information in the contact and reservation register by logging in to your user profile at norge.no

If you have any questions, contact the ID port user support (+47) 800 30 300.

Email

brukerstotte@digdir.no

NB! Do not send sensitive information about yourself and your electronic ID by email.
