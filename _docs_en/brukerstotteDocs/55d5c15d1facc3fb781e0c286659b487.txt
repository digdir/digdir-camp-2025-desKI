How to log in with BankID with app

To log in with BankID with app you need:

birth number
code app from your bank 
personal password

Go to the service you want to log in to (eg at: www.nav.no, www.altinn.no, www.skatteaten.no)

Select BankID in the ID port
Enter Birth Number, 11 digits
Select the login "BankID with app"
Approve the alert from the app on your mobile
Enter personal password

Note that BankID must be activated at your bank to use it in the ID port.

BankID provides access to public services at the highest security level (level 4).
