What happens to the mailbox at death?

E-Box: In the event of death, e-box has the right to delete the user's e-box after 15 months. Relatives authorized to manage the estate must contact e-box to access the mailbox of the deceased.
 

Digipost: In the event of death, the Digipost account is deleted after 12 months. Relatives who want access can send a change certificate to Digipost Customer Service within nine months to access the account.
