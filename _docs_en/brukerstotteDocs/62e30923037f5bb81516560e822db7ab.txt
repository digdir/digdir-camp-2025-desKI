How to change your user profile

If you want to change your mobile number or email address, you can do this on your user profile. Here you can also choose digital mailbox, reserve yourself, cancel reservation and change language choices.
The information is collected in one joint contact register for the state and municipality. Read more.

Log in to get to your user profile.

To change your registered information:

• Click "Change"
• Once you have entered the correct information, click "Save"
• Press “Exit and log out” at the bottom of the page
