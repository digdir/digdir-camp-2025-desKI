Not received minid activation letter

MinID activation letter is only sent to the address you have registered in the National Register. If you have both postal address and residential address, the letter is sent to the postal address.

If you have not received mini-activation letters within 10 working days, we recommend that you contact the ID port user support on phone number (+47) 800 30 300 or start a chat with us.
