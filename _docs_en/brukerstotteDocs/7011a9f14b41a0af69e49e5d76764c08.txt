What does it mean that digital mail is sent safely?

To ensure that unauthorized persons cannot read your mail, it is sent encrypted to your digital mailbox. An encrypted broadcast can be compared to putting on a padlock. Only the one with the key can unlock and read the content.

When you get mail digitally from public senders, you must log in via the ID port to read your mail.
