Electronic notification

When the state or municipality sends you a decision or other letter with important content, you will be notified on the mobile number or email address you entered into the contact and reservation register. It is equally important that the correct contact information is registered in the contact and reservation register, as the correct residential address is registered in the National Register.

Your contact information can also be used by the state and municipality to send reminders, information and the like. For example, there may be reminder of agreements, or notice of water closure.
