Why do I get an email about creating a digital mailbox when I have already done this?

When public companies send out e-mails with a call to create a digital mailbox, they use the contact information contained in the contact and reservation register.

If you have registered your email address on another person, it is you who get the email with the call. You may have helped a family member or are guardian. In this context, you may have provided your email address. This can be, for example, when logging in to the tax return.
