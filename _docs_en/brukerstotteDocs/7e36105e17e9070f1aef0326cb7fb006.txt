Not receiving one -time code on sms

If you do not get code on SMS, try to turn on and off your mobile. Then start a new login.

Do you remember your password and are registered with the correct email address? 

Then you can do the following:

Download MinID app
Enter the birth number
Enter password
You will now come up with a code on SMS to you. Click "Send Email"
Enter code received by email
You have now become a minid app uses and will receive notice in the app when you log in

Close MinID app. Open a browser:

Go to the service you would like to log in to
Choose MinID
Enter the birth number and password
You will now receive a notice in Minid app that you need to approve
You are now logged in

Are you registered with the wrong phone number? Here you can change your contact information. 

If you do not get it to work, contact us at (+47) 800 30 300 or chat, and we will help you further.
