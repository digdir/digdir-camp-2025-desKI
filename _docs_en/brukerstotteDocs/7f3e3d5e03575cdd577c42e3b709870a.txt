Can I change mailbox if I regret it?

Yes, you can switch digital mailbox at any time. You do this by logging in to
"Update contact information".

Once you have logged in to "Your Contact Information", you will see which mailbox you have selected. Click the pencil icon to change the digital mailbox. If you have not selected Digital Mailbox before, you can do so from this page.

It is easy to move the mail you have received from the public to your new mailbox.
