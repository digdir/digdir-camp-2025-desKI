Duplicate contact information

The mobile number and/or e-mail address you are trying to register are also registered with other persons in the contact and reservation register. You should check if the contact information is correct.

The contact information that is registered is the information that the public will use if you are to be notified.  If it is conscious that the contact information is registered with more than one person, you must be aware that the public notice will not always reach the intended one.
