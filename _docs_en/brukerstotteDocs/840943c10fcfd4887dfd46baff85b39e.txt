What is esignation

Electronic signature (E-signature) is your personal, electronic signature.
The signing service offers you to use an advanced electronic signature when signing public documents and applications.

Advanced electronic signature is an electronic signature that:

a) is unambiguously linked to the signer,

b) can identify the signer,

c) is made using funds that only the signer has control over, and

d) are associated with other electronic data in such a way that it can be detected if these have been changed after signing

BankID and Buypass ID can be used for advanced electronic signature in the signing service.
