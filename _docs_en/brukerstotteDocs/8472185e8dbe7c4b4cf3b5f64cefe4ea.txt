How to record MinID user with app

To register as a mini-user, you need the following:

Birth number or D-number
Mobile phone and email address
Minide activation letter

If you are missing a mini -activation letter, you can order it here. MinID activation letter is valid for 60 days after ordering. 

MinID activation letter is only sent to the address you have registered in the Norwegian National Register. If you have both a postal address and a residential address, it will be sent to the postal address.

Normal delivery time for minid activation letters are 10 business days (inside Norway).

 

To register MinID user with app, do the following:

Open the Minide app
Click "Get Started"
Enter birth number. Click "Next"
Scan the activation code (QR) with camera. NB! You must allow camera to use the app
Create a personal password
Enter mobile number. Click "Next"
Enter email address. Click "Next"
You are now registered as Minid App uses. Next time you log in you can use your phone to approve login


The password must satisfy the following requirements: 

must be a minimum of 8 characters long
must consist of letters and numbers
