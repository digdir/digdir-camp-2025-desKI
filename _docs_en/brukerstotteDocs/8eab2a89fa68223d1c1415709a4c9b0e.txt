Privacy statement
The privacy statement is about how the Norwegian Digitalisation Agency collects and uses information in the contact and reservation register and Digital Mailbox.

The declaration contains an overview of what you have requirements to access when information is collected about you. This applies to information on how we process personal information, disclosure of personal data to other public companies and your dishes related to access, correction and deletion.

Privacy Ombudsman:
If you have questions about personal information in the common solutions, such as changing language choices, please contact brukerstotte@digdir.no. The Privacy Ombudsman can be contacted if you have other types of privacy questions: Privacy ombud@digdir.no
