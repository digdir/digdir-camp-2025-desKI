Privacy Statement for MinID

Processing

The Manager is the Directorate of Digitization, org.no. 991 825 827. Digdir can be contacted by phone (+47) 800 30 300, usstotte@digdir.no, or at chat http://eid.difi.no/nb.

Privacy Ombudsman is reached at privacy ombud@dir.no.
 

Purpose, treatment basis and recipients

Digdir processes personal information about you to issue an electronic ID - MinID, and to use it to authenticate you with services that accept MinID as authentication. Treatment basis is the conditions for the use of minid according to GDPR Art 6 letter b.

The birth number is shared with the services that have a basis for treatment for it and need it to ensure unequivocal identification of you.

We also remind you of the registered right to access, correction, and in some cases deletion, according to GDPR Chapter III.
 

Information processed

Digdir processes the following information to identify you, cf. section 3.1 above:

Birth number or D-number
Name and Populated Postal Address (Deleted When Activation Code Letter is used)
Mobile number (shared with the contact and reservation register)
E-mail address (shared with the contact and reservation register)

As part of the safety, Digdir records the times you log in with MinID, as well as IP address and other information about the device from which you use mini-ID. Logging in login is deleted after 13 months.

When logging in, cookies that are further described here: https://eid.difi.no/nb/sikkyness-og- Privacy
