The contact and reservation register
The state and municipality must have access to your contact information to send you messages and documents electronically.  This information is collected in the contact and reservation register.

Some other companies that perform tasks on behalf of the public can also use the register, but only for the part of the business that applies to the public assignment. Read more at the collaborative portal.

The contact and reservation register may contain:

your mobile number and email address
information on which digital mailbox you have selected
Language choice for electronic communication
If you are reserved for electronic communication

It is the Directorate of Digitization that is responsible for the contact and reservation register.  

The provisions of the information can be found in the Regulations on Electronic Communication with and in the Administration (the Emergency Regulations) § 29 and 31.

Here you can log in to check what is registered on you and update your contact information

As a citizen, you can announce that you would rather make decisions and important letters on paper. You do this by reserving yourself against digital communication.
