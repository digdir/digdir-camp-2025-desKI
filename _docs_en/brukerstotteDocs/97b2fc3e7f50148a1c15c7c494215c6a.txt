Can I choose to return to mail on paper from the public?

Yes, if you want to go back to getting mail from the public on paper again, you need to reserve yourself against getting mail digitally. You can go to www.norge.no and reserve yourself, or call the ID port support for 800 30 300.

If you also choose to end your digital mailbox, you can save the documents on your own computer.
