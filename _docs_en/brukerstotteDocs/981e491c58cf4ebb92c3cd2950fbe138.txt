Receives not notice in the MinID app
If you do not receive notice in the app, you can check the following:
Have you allowed notifications in the app? You can check this by going to settings on your phone and search for the MinID app.
Is the app updated to the latest version? 
Is the phone updated to the latest version? 
Is your phone connected to the Internet?
