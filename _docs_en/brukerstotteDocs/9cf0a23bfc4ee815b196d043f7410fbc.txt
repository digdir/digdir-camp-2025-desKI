Allow cookies in safari

You must allow Safari to store cookies on your iOS device in order to log in with the ID port.

To allow cookies, do the following:

Open "Settings"
Choose "Safari"
Remove "Block all info capsules"
Email

brukerstotte@digdir.no

NB! Do not send sensitive information about yourself and your electronic ID by email.
