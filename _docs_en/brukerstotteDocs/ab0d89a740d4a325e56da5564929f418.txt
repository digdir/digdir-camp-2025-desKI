What you can use your electronic ID for
Electronic ID gives you access to public services on the Internet that requires login.

With online services from the state and municipality you can, for example

Calculate your pension at nav.no
Seek scholarship at Lånskassen.no
Report moving at skatteetaten.no
Change your tax card at skatteetaten.no 
read, right and deliver your tax return

Public services on the Internet provide you with safe access to communicate with public companies.
