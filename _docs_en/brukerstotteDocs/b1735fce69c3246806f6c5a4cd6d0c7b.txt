Collaboration

The collaborative portal, http://so cooperation.digdir.no is the primary information and notification channel from the Norwegian Digitalisation Agency on the collaboration around the ID port.

Access to the collaborative portal is awarded to a service owner when entering into a cooperation agreement for the ID port. The collaborative portal includes updated technical documentation, operating information, contact information, meeting summons/minutes and relevant reports and statistics.

For questions about the collaborative portal, send an e-mail to serviceedesk@igdir.no
