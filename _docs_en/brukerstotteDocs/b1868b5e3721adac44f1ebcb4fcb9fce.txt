How do I reserve myself?

You can reserve yourself by clicking on this link. You need to log in to create a reservation. If you are not allowed to log in, you can contact user support for help with entering reservation. 

If you reserve against digital communication, you will receive important letters from the public only on paper.

It is illegal to reserve others without their consent, and it can be punishable in accordance with section 8 of the Personal Data Act. 

If you are registered in the contact and reservation register, you will receive a receipt by e-mail or SMS when you have been reserved with the wording: "You have reserved yourself for communication online with state and municipality. Call us if this does not match, the ID port 800 30 300".

If you want more information or help to reserve you, call tel. (+47) 800 30 300 or see www.norge.no
