"Incorrect data entered"

The error message "The wrong data entered" may come when you enter the one -time code from the code generator during login.

This error message means that your BankID is not activated for public services, but can only be used for logging in to your online bank.

You can enable BankID for public services by logging into your online bank, selecting BankID in the menu and then ordering or activating BankID.

If you need help creating or activating BankID, contact your bank.
