How to log in with Commfides

To log in with Commfides you need:

Commfides e-ID
PIN

How to log in: 

Go to the service you want to log in to (eg: www.nav.no, www.altinn.no, www.skatteaten.no)
Download Commfides Plug In
Select Commfides
Insert Commfides e-ID
Enter PIN code
Click "Identify" and log in

Commfides provides access to public services at the highest level of security (level 4).
