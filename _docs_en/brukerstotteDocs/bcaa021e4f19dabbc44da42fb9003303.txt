The courses below are developed by the Norwegian Digitalisation Agency.

The training is intended for course leaders and others who want to offer training for people who have not used the Internet so much and want to learn more. You can download and use all the courses for free.

If you need training resources for basic digital competence, you will find this on digidel.no.
