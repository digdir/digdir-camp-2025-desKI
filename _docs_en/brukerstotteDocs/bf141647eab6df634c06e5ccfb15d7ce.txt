Check that you are registered with the correct mobile number and email
To communicate with you digitally, the public must have access to the correct mobile number and email address.

It is important that you have registered your own contact information in the contact and reservation register. If your mobile number or email address is registered with several people, you must be aware that you will also receive notice that applies to these. 

Log in to check which mobile number and email address you are registered with.

If you have any questions about your contact information, please contact the user support for the ID port by phone: 800 30 300
