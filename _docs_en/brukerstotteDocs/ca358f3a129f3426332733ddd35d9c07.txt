How to log in with MinID app
Go to Google Play or Apple Store
Seek up "Minid app"
Download the app
Remember to accept that the app gets access to notifications
Register phone: Fill in social security number. Click "Next"
Up in passwords. Click "Next"
Post the code received by SMS. Click "Next"

Next time you log in with MinID you can use the app instead of SMS

Go to the service you want to log in to (eg at: www.nav.no, www.altinn.no, www.skatteaten.no)
Choose MinID
Enter your birth number or D number and your password. Click "Next"
You will now receive a push notification on your phone. Confirm this using fingerprints, face recognition or personal pin associated with your phone

MinID provides access to public services at a significant level of security (level 3).
