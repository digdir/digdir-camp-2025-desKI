How to Order Minide Activation Letter

Only new MinID users can order activation letters.

Follow these steps to order minid activation letters:

Go to MinID activation letter
Enter the birth number or D-number. Click "Order activation letter"

MinID activation letter is only sent by mail to your registered address. If you have both postal address and residential address, the letter is sent to the postal address.

Normal delivery time for minid activation letter is 10 business days.

MinID activation letters can be ordered from the year you turn 13.

MinID activation letter is valid for 60 days and you will not be able to place a new order during this period. Contact the ID port support if you need a new order before 60 days have passed.
