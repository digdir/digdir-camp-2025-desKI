"Warning: You have been blocked due to repeated errors in login"

If you have entered the wrong password or one -time code 3 times, your password will be blocked for an hour. If you click on "Forgot Password", you can create a new password right away and thus do not have to wait an hour before you can log in again.

MinID counts the number of incorrect logins from the last successful login, and the census is not removed until you have had a successful login.
