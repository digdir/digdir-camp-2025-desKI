"System failure"
The error message often comes when using a favorite link or a bookmark to get right to the website.
The error can occur if you use navigation buttons in the browser (back and forth) during login.
If you have been automatically chopped due to inactivity, you can also get this error. You must then restart the login.
In some cases, you need to delete cookies in your browser. Guidance on how to do it can be found here
