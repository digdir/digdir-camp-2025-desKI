How to create new password for MinID app
Open Minid app
Click the person in the upper right corner
Click "Get New Password"/"Change Password"
Enter the birth number or D-number. Click "Next"
Enter the code received by email, 5 characters. Click "Next"
Enter the code received by SMS, 5 characters. Click "Next"
Create new password, at least 8 characters (allowed characters are A-Å, 0-9 and special characters)
You can now log in with your new password. Go to the service you wish to log in to
