How do I get a digital mailbox?

The public has entered into an agreement with two suppliers of digital mailbox, digipost and e-box. You choose the mailbox you like best.

To create digital mailbox:

Go to the website www.norge.no
Click "Select Digital Mailbox"
Read the information and select e-box or digipost
To create a digital mailbox from e-box or digipost, you must identify yourself electronically with BankID, Buypass ID or Commfides

If you have trouble creating the digital mailbox you want, you can send an email or call the user support to the mailbox supplier you selected.

Digipost: kundeservice@digipost.no or tel. (+47) 22 03 00 00

e-box: Contact form or tel. (+47) 22 89 85 04
