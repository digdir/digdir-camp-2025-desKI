What happens if I forget to check the digital mailbox?

When you receive digital mail from the public, you will be notified by SMS or e-mail.

Post delivered to your digital mailbox is left there.

You are responsible for familiarizing yourself with the contents of the mail sent to you, in the same way as when you get one paper letter.

Answer and complaints are valid from the day you are notified and can open your mail, in the same way as mail on paper.
