Handling of business certificate
When you receive the business certificate (P12 format) from Buypass or Commfides you get it in 2 or 3 parts, as well as associated passwords. What is to be used is the authentication section.
The certificate must be entered into a JKS (Java Key Store) and it is done as follows:

Open Key Store Explorer.
Press 'File' and 'Open'. Navigate to where the certificate is open.
Press 'Tools' and then 'Change Keystore type'. Choose JKS.
Press 'File' and 'Save AS'. Save the file as name.jks. 
Example: Minketore.jks
Check that the file is stored as .jks, not .ks!

We recommend that you keep the password that came with the certificate.
Export of Public Key
As part of the authentication, Digdir must have Public Key of the certificate. 
It can be exported in Key Store Explorer:

Open the jks you just made in Key Store Explorer.
Right -click the certificate located in the keyor, find 'Export' and then press 'Export Certificate Chain'.
Leave everything as it is and save as a .CER file. 
Example: Mitt -Public Certificate.Cer


Then send the file to serviceedesk@dir.no.
