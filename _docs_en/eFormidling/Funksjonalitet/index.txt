Mass broadcast to residents
A business may need to communicate with a larger amount of residents. The communication needs can be triggered by
dates (eg sending out election cards), events (eg birth) or other. Anything may be needed from annual
Mass broadcasts to completely dynamic volume with automated messages. Both parts are supported by eFonsidation. Both the simple
And more composite patterns of use are supported by mass envoy to residents.

Machine-to-machine communication between businesses
One company's systems may need to communicate with another company's systems without people being involved.
Such communication is usually characterized with highly structured content. Examples may include dissemination of
Events (eg birth) and information collection (eg statistics). e -insight is an example of use
Of e-handling to machine-to-machine communication and another is the support for the protocols in FIKS IO. co -operation
Also supports "lightweight" protocols that are agreed directly between those who are to interact and who do not require coordination
with or adaptation of eFonsidling.
