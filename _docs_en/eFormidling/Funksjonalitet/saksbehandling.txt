Introduction
Case handling is the process from a triggering event to a decision. Possible triggering events include to
send an application or to have children. The Administration Act defines a decision as a decision made during the exercise of
public authority and which generally or specifically determines the rights or obligations of private persons
(Individuals or other private legal subjects). Procedure rules for public authorities are found in
The Public Administration Act with adjustments from regulations for different areas such as child welfare.
Public enterprises usually use case processing systems to facilitate effective case processing and
compliance with case processing rules. Many of the case processing systems contain support for record keeping and
Archiving and is often called case and archive systems. There are also many case processing systems adapted to specific
Areas (for example, child welfare) that are often called professional systems.
As part of the case processing, communication between several companies will often be needed. Example includes:
- Obtaining documentation
- Complaints processing
- Quality assurance
In these cases, it is usually desirable that communication goes directly between the business processing system
So that the documents sent and received can be automatically linked to the case in question. the support of the e -officing for
Case management provides effective communication about cases between different case management systems. Emsyring can also be used for
to facilitate (part-) automated case processing.

What processes a recipient supports are up to the recipient and is shown in the recipient's capabilities. The recipient can on
Its side choose to handle different processes differently. For example, by routing the messages to different subject systems or to different
Work lists in the same system. Usually a business acts both as the sender and the recipient, but it is also possible to
Just act as a sender.

Address messages
Messages are addressed from the sender's organization number to the recipient's organization number.
For recipients who use e -dissemination themselves, the Emsyring's messaging service is preferred. For recipients who do not use
Emypesing is preferred KS Svarut if the recipient uses this service. For all other recipients preferred
Altinn digital mail.

Notification
Sender System receives status messages when a message is delivered. Sender's system also receives status messages at
error situations and when a message is not delivered within the defined life. Sender's system can notify
The sender of both successful broadcasts and deviations.
In addition, E-Mozening sends an email notice of any deviations to the sender's defined notification point. Notification is collected
and is sent periodically.
Upon receipt to the subject system, the receiving system may make notification of receiving a message so that the message does not risk to
stay unread.
Upon receipt to Altinn Digital mail, the company can be notified of reception of notification. Notification goes to business
Registered notification address in the Unit Register.
