Introduction
The Public Administration Act defines a decision as a decision made during the exercise of public authority and as
in general or specifically determining the rights or obligations of private persons (individuals or other
private legal entities). A decision is the result of a case processing. The Administration Act makes demands on
Content of a decision and how a decision should be communicated. the euthority regulations and the digitization circular
places further requirements on how a decision should be communicated digitally.
Notification requirements:

The business shall use contact information from the contact and reservation register when notification of residents of and
Submission of individual decisions and other important digital inquiries, cf. section 8 of the Emergency Regulations.
Digitization Circular (external link)
The information system should record the time when the party has obtained access to the individual decision, and data
confirming that he or she has the right to familiarize himself with the decision. Has the party not obtained access to
The single decision within one week from the time the decision was made available and notice was sent, the party shall
is notified again in accordance with the third paragraph.
The Emergency Regulations (external link)

Requirements for the use of digital mail to residents:

Business should use digital mailbox for residents for sending mail to residents who have chosen digital
Mailbox, and which has not reserved. The requirement for the use of digital mailbox to residents applies all services
where letters are sent that have documentation value for the inhabitant. Such letters can be both decisions and other important
Inquiries. The company will consider which letters have important documentation value for the inhabitant.
Digitization Circular (external link)

Possibility to use Altinn Digital Post for residents without a digital mailbox:

Companies that, by establishing a digital mailbox to residents, used Altinn's message box for sending
Post to residents, from October 1, 2016, would use a digital mailbox for residents. Has the inhabitant not chosen
Mailbox and not reserved for digital mail, mail can still be sent to Altinn's message box.
Digitization Circular (external link)

Message content
The content that can be sent depends on which document types the recipient supports for decisions to the citizen. One
Document type is supported by one or more messaging services.
| Document Type | Message Services |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Digital | Digital mail to residents |
| Print | Digital mail to residents |
| Digital DPV | Altinn Digital Post |
Address messages
Messages are addressed from the sender's organization number to the recipient's birth number. For prints, the recipient's birth number can be omitted if not known or if the recipient does not have a birth number.
For recipients who have selected Digital Mailbox, this is preferred. For recipients who have not selected mailbox must sender
Even choose whether the message can be sent to Altinn Digital mail or whether it must be sent as physical mail. Recipients that are
Reserved for digital mail or which cannot be notified due to lack of contact information should in all cases
Receive physical mail. When sending physical mail, the sender may choose to use the printing service in digital mail to
Residents or handle these messages with their own printing service.

Notification
Sender System receives status messages when a message is delivered. Sender's system also receives status messages at
error situations and when a message is not delivered within the defined life. Sender's system can notify
The sender of both successful broadcasts and deviations.
Upon receipt of digital mail to residents, the citizen can be notified of reception of notification. Notification goes to the inhabitant's
Registered contact information in the contact and reservation register.
Upon reception to Altinn Digital mail, the citizen can be notified of reception of notification. Notification goes to the inhabitant's
Registered contact information in the contact and reservation register.
