Emsyring offers several tools that allow a troubleshooter e -dissemination:

The integration point offers support services below. Feel free to see:
Spring Boot's support services (external link)
Integration point produces log (both to file and stdout)
The integration point sends logs to eForsing central log service so that digdir can troubleshoot across
Sender's integration point, recipient's integration point and e -dissemination's central services
The Integration Point's interface offers services to retrieve message metadata including the status of messages
Emsynings Central Services offers email notification for messages that fail and expire, and the expense certificate expires

Most error situations that arise are well -known and outside of e -officing control such as network problems,
Downtime and other error situations of the messaging services used, discontinued certificate of sender/recipient, etc.
