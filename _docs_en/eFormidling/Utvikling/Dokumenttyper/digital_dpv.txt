Standard Business Document
All document types in e -forming follow the standard of addressing information.  consists of a header ()
And a business message used for document type-specific addressing information.
The use of the standard is described on the Standard Business Document.
The document type is addressed from the sender's organization number to the recipient's birth number.

One or more documents
The types of documents supported depend on the recipient and are not known in advance. This means that attempts to
Sending documents that are not supported by the recipient fails first upon reception.
File format that is supported: HTML and attachment with arbitrary format.
