The document type consists of a standard Business Document (SBD).
The document type has the identifier.
The document type is sent in response to a publication.
The document type is always sent with the process, regardless of which process
The original publication had.

Standard Business Document
All document types in e -forming follow the standard of addressing information.  consists of a header ()
And a business message used for document type-specific addressing information.
The use of the standard is described on the Standard Business Document.
The document type is addressed from the sender's organization number to Figdir's organization number.
