Standard Business Document
All document types in e -forming follow the standard of addressing information.  consists of a header ()
And a business message used for document type-specific addressing information.
The use of the standard is described on the Standard Business Document.
The document type is addressed from GIGDIR's organization number to the recipient's organization number.
