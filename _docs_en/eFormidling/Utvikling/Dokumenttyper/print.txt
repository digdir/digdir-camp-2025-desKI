Standard Business Document
All document types in e -forming follow the standard of addressing information.  consists of a header ()
And a business message used for document type-specific addressing information.
The use of the standard is described on the Standard Business Document.
The document type is addressed from the sender's organization number to the recipient's birth number. If the recipients
The birth number is unknown this can be left out. In such cases, it is required to explicitly state the recipient's postal address, because
Otherwise, eFonsling does not have enough information to do address lookups. One can thus use to send physical mail
to foreign residents and businesses.

Recommendations by addressing
In order to reduce the risk of letter mail not reaching the recipient, Posten has made good recommendations on how to address properly, both for recipients in Norway and abroad. It is recommended that all sender companies follow these. Read more on the mail addressing (external link)
Important notes of error status on print messages:
Although a printout message has been granted, it can nevertheless fail at a later date. It can happen in the following cases:
The message fails during the print job itself. This will normally happen within 24 hours after the message has been delivered. Please note that the printing service does not run on holidays and holidays! It may therefore take more than 24 hours for a message to fail in this scenario. 
The message fails as a result of a return post, ie it cannot be delivered to the physical postal address of the inhabitant. An error status due to return mail will in most cases be returned within a period of two weeks. In rarer cases, it may take up to four weeks for a letter to return. Note that there are only messages with print.Retur.Returhavning set for shredding_med_melding that will receive error status on the return post.
Sender must be able to handle a message may fail after the status delivered, and it is therefore recommended to check the status for a period after it is marked as delivered.
Tip: To retrieve more details as to why a message has failed, you can decode the Raw Receipten that comes with the status message. View Raw Receipt
