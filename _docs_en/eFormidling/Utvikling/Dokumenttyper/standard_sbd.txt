Emsyring includes one standard Business Document (SBD) as part of all messages. SBD consists of a header (sbdh) and
A business message. SBDH contains information for identification, addressing and routing the message.
The business message contains additional information depending on which document type is sent. Each of
The document types in e -mediation have an associated business message. Feel free to see the document types in the eFonsening at:

Business message
The business message contains extra addressing information and instructions depending on the document type.
SBD (including the business message) is not encrypted so that it can be used for routing and decision -making by
Processing of the message until reception.
Each document type has its own business message. See the individual document type for details:
