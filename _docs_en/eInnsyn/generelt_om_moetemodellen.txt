Generally about the meeting model
The meeting data model is based on as it is in Noark 5. This model is very simplified in relation to earlier Noark versions and is based on some
Classes such as Arvar registration and folder classes in the general noark model.
A general adaptation has been made.  The type of meeting registration is divided into meeting documents registration and meeting case registration. Meeting document registration is
Typical occurrences of notice, protocols, etc. While meeting case registration is the actual case presentation

The use of the model

Priority of meetings upon sending
(Note! Own in-queue for meetings will cease 1 January 2024)
In reception at e-insight, a separate in-queue has been created for meetings. This is to increase the flow of meeting delivery.
In order to use this, when sending the integration point, one must change the process type to meeting, where there is today a journal entry.
