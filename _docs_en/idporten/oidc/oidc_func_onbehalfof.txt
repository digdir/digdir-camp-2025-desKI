Summary: "Onbehalfof allows a supplier to reuse an OIDC integration on behalf of many customers."
Sidebar: Oidc
Product: ID port
redirect_From: /oidc_func_onbehalfof

About the functionality
Onbehalfof makes it possible for service providers to offer services to different customers over the same integration.

Provides opportunity to have different names, logo and back-url
Statistics / Billing goes to the correct service owner

Note that an Onbehalfof integration does not provide access to Scopes that its own customer has accessed.
