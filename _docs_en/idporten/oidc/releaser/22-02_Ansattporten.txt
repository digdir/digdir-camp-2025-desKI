Simple organizational voter for Altinn Authorization
Scheduled production setting: Feb 15, 2022
New functionality:
Generic employee logging with Altinn (in Design)
Introduce a rich organizational voter in the employee gate, where logged in the user can choose which organization they want to represent for the relevant login.
The services must configure what role/representation from Altinn authorization they need.
For data sharing scenario, it will also be possible to get access_tokens with the link between logged in user and selected organization.
