On this page, I try to give an overall explanation of the architecture of the wallet and the sandbox.
Through revision of the Eidas Regulation, the EU Member States will be required to:
- Offer a digital identity wallet to all residents and businesses by November 2026
- the wallet should be free to get and use for residents
- the wallet should be able to use for login
- Pocket Book should be able to use to exchange evidence (such as driver's license, vaccination card, company certificate, etc.) for services
The EU wallet ecosystem is largely inspired by technology for decentralized identity and own control over data through the use of the Sokalla Verifiable Credentials, as shown in the figure below:

The European Commission publishes a reference architecture, the so-called "Arf", which depicts the ecosystem and roles and processes included. Me recommends that those who are interested in sitting deeper into architecture read this.
One can note that the arf is not formally a legally binding document, it is the law and the acts (implementing acts) that are, and these are, in turn, point to adopted standards from ETSI, ISO and Openid Foundation.  But in practice, it is the arf that gave the best picture of how things were connected.

In order for this ecosystem to function, the components depend on a common confidence framework that determines the woman who is approved players.  These confidence lists are in the process of being established by the European Commission as central European joint services.  Since the Norwegian sandbox will already work in 2025, which is earlier than the central registries from the EU are completed, but also legally be independent of the introduction of the Eidas law in Norway through the EEA process, Digdir will offer a Norwegian sandbox-specific trust framework that must be used:

The trust framework in the sandbox



As a result of registration on the confidence list, the player is given a certificate to be used for authentication in the case of interaction. The interaction itself is based on two closely related protocols from the OpenID Foundation, namely OpenID4VCI to make a evidence or open time4VP to use a evidence.
