Digdir offers a national "sandbox" for digital wallet where anyone who wants to explore opportunities and experiment in an isolated and safe test environment. 
You can read more about the overall framework and conditions for participating in the sandbox, as well as other related projects in Digdir, on the Digital wallet project site.
On these pages you will find technical documentation on the sandbox. 
Me recommends that you start by reading more about the architecture of the sandbox, before taking a look at an overview of services that are found in the sandbox as of no.
When you are ready to participate and create your own services in the sandbox, you have to get into how you make a proof or use evidence.
