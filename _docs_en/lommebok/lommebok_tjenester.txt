On this page you will find links to the most useful service that is part of the sandbox.   
Digdir is working on creating a web-based access service that makes it possible to see what services are registered in the trust list.
Issue

DEGDIR DEMO issuer.  This is a simple demo service that can issue a PID document, a proxy certificate and a daily duty certificate.  You can also look at the protocol flow.
https://issuer.eudiw.dev/.  The European Commission offers a relatively technically oriented issuer of PID and mobile driver's license. It is not integrated with the sandbox's confidence list, but is useful to use in your own development.

Digdir Demo Brukarstad Here you can test age verification based on PID, and proxy login based on proof of authority.  You can also study the protocol flow.
The ID port in the test environment for the ID port, it is possible to log in with a PID document issued by our demo issuer.
